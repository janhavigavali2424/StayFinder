Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how registration, admin verification, category filtering, and booking work on StayFinder.

How does Property Registration & Approval work?

When an owner registers a property on Registration.html, it goes directly to our Admin Verification Control Desk (Status: Pending Approval). The Admin reviews it and clicks Approve, which immediately makes the property visible publicly on the Home Page!

How can I filter properties by Category?

On the Home Page, you will find interactive category pills: Boys PG, Girls PG, Co-Living, Private Apartment, and Luxury Studio. Clicking any pill filters the live listing grid instantly without refreshing the page.

Is there any brokerage or commission fee?

No, StayFinder is a direct portal connecting students and property owners. Browsing verified stays is 100% free for students.

Where can I see my owner listings status?

Owners can visit the Owner Dashboard to check approval statuses, view tenant messages, and track listing performance.
